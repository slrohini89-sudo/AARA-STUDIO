## 1. Objective of Project Rules

The project rules establish transparent expectations and professional boundaries from Day 1. Every freelance design engagement is clearly defined prior to starting so both Riva Visuals and the client are aligned on deliverables, timelines, and limits.

---

## 2. Core Project Parameters (The 5 Defined Pillars)

Every project agreement explicitly specifies:
1. **Number of Designs:** The exact quantity of visual creatives agreed upon (e.g., 6 posts).
2. **Revision Limit:** Exactly 1–2 revisions per design (agreed upon at kickoff). **No unlimited revisions.**
3. **Specific Delivery Timeline:** An exact agreed turnaround date/window determined per project (no generic one-size-fits-all timeline).
4. **Final Files Included:** High-resolution, ready-to-post graphics (e.g., JPG/PNG in platform-optimized dimensions).
5. **Out-of-Scope Handling:** Any request outside the agreed scope requires confirmation and a separate charge before work is carried out.

---

## 3. Revisions Policy

* **Included in Project:** 1–2 rounds of revisions per design, as agreed in the initial quote.
* **What a Revision Covers:** Adjustments to typography, color balance, positioning, and text tweaks based on the original brief.
* **Excess Revisions:** Any revision beyond the agreed number is treated as additional work and requires an extra charge confirmed before execution.
* **Absolute Rule:** Unlimited revisions are strictly not offered.

---

## 4. Project Scope Definition

A project scope outlines exactly what the client is paying for.

### Example Project Scope:
* **Project Name:** 6 Promotional Instagram Posts
* **Included Deliverables:**
  * Exactly 6 custom visual designs
  * Agreed revision limit (e.g., 1 revision per design)
  * Agreed delivery timeframe (e.g., 4–5 days)
  * Final ready-to-post high-resolution files
* **Not Included:** Any additional designs, unagreed alternate design concepts, or extra revision rounds.

---

## 5. Delivery Timeline Guidelines

* **Project-Specific Turnaround:** Every timeline is set individually based on project complexity and quantity before work begins.
* **Agreed Schedule:** Work starts only after the 50% advance is received, and the delivery timeline begins from that date.
* **Drafts vs. Final:** Initial drafts are shared first for review; final files follow after revision sign-off and balance payment.

---

## 6. Out-of-Scope Work Workflow

When a client asks for additional creatives or substantial redesigns beyond the original agreement:
1. **Acknowledge Politely:** Recognize the request without being defensive.
2. **Clarify Scope Boundary:** Clearly state that this addition falls outside the current project scope.
3. **Confirm & Charge Separately:** Quote the additional work and obtain written approval before doing any of the work.
4. **No Silent Additions:** Never execute extra work for free or without prior agreement.
