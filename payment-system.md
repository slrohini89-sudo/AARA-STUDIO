# Riva Visuals — Payment System (Stage 6)
**Brand:** Riva Visuals  
**Title:** Freelance Social Media Designer  
**Scope:** Stage 6 Freelance Payment Workflow & Tracking

---

## 1. Core Payment Policy

To maintain professional standards and protect both parties:
* **Standard Projects:** **50% Advance Payment** is required before design work starts. The remaining **50% Balance** is due after designs and agreed revisions are finalized, before final high-resolution files are handed over.
* **Very Small Jobs:** For very small, single-item tasks (e.g., a single story or simple post under ₹500), 100% upfront payment is requested prior to starting.
* **Work Initiation Rule:** Work begins **only** after the advance is confirmed.
* **File Release Rule:** Final high-resolution assets are delivered **only** after the remaining balance is confirmed.

---

## 2. Standard 7-Step Payment Flow

```
1. Client confirms project scope & quote
               ↓
2. Client pays 50% advance
               ↓
3. Riva Visuals starts design work
               ↓
4. Initial designs are shared for review
               ↓
5. Agreed revisions are completed (1 revision per design)
               ↓
6. Client transfers remaining 50% balance
               ↓
7. Final high-resolution files are delivered
```

---

## 3. Project Payment Breakdown (Example: ₹2,000 Project)

| Milestone | Percentage | Amount | Trigger / Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Total Project Value** | 100% | **₹2,000** | Agreed scope (e.g., 6 promotional posts) |
| **Milestone 1 (Advance)** | 50% | **₹1,000** | Required to schedule and start design work |
| **Milestone 2 (Balance)** | 50% | **₹1,000** | Required after designs are approved to release final files |
