## 4. Personalized Cold-DM Outreach Framework

*Strict Rule: No generic messages or spam. Every outreach is concise, respectful, and personalized.*

### The 4-Part Structure

| Part | Component | Script Template |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Personal Observation** | *"I came across your page and really liked your [product/service]."* |
| **2** | **Identify Opportunity** | *"I noticed your Instagram could have more consistent promotional creatives."* |
| **3** | **Explain What I Do** | *"I'm a freelance social-media designer and I create posts, carousels, stories and promotional posters for businesses."* |
| **4** | **Call to Action** | *"Would you be interested in seeing some of my work?"* |

## 5. Daily Client-Acquisition Routine (Part-Time: 1–2 Hours/Day, Mon–Fri)

### Daily Schedule Breakdown

* **Block 1 (30 Mins) — Find Prospects:**
  * Identify 10–15 relevant businesses on Instagram.
  * Log them into the tracking sheet.

* **Block 2 (30 Mins) — Outreach:**
  * Send 10–15 personalized DMs using the 4-part structure.

* **Block 3 (30 Mins) — Portfolio / Design Improvement:**
  * Refine the 10 portfolio pieces, create sample visual assets, or practice new creative styles.

### Prospect Tracking Sheet Format

| Business | Instagram Handle | Contacted (Date) | Reply (Y/N) | Follow-up (Date) | Result / Next Step |

## 7. Operational Mindset & Integrity Rules

* **Focus on Input, Not Vanity Metrics:** Do not obsess over follower counts. Track tangible business metrics: Prospects, Outreach, Replies, Conversations, Clients.
* **Volume Perspective:** A small batch of non-replies (e.g., 10–15 messages) is statistically normal and not an indicator that the freelance model fails.
* **Strict Role Boundaries:**
  * Deliver visual/design content only.
  * Do NOT offer or mention social media management, social media strategy, marketing strategy, or content management.
  * Do NOT make guarantees regarding follower growth, engagement rates, or sales figures.
