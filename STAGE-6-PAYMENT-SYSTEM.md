---

### 1. Payment Policy
* **Standard Projects:** A **50% Advance Payment** is required before any design work begins. The remaining **50% Balance** is paid after the agreed work and revisions are completed, before the final high-resolution files are delivered.
* **Work Rule:** Under no circumstances does design work begin before the 50% advance payment is received and confirmed.
* **Delivery Rule:** Final high-resolution, ready-to-post files are delivered only after the remaining 50% payment is cleared.

---

### 2. 50% Advance Explanation
The 50% advance payment secures the project on the calendar and protects Riva Visuals by ensuring the client is committed. It covers the initial design phase and concept creation. No drafts or concepts are created without this advance.

---

### 3. Remaining Payment Explanation
The remaining 50% payment is requested once:
1. The initial designs have been created and reviewed.
2. The agreed revisions (1 revision per design) have been fully completed.
3. The client approves the design previews.
Once the remaining payment is received, the high-resolution, ready-to-use files are sent immediately.

---

| Component | Percentage | Amount | Requirement / Trigger |

---

### 5. Small-Project Upfront Payment Rule
For very small jobs (e.g., a single story, single post, or small design under ₹500):
* 100% payment is requested upfront before starting.
* This keeps the process simple and avoids splitting very small amounts into two transactions.

### 7. Clear Payment Status Tracking

Use this tracker for every client project to instantly know whether work can begin and whether final files can be released:

| Tracking Parameter | Project Example | Operational Decision Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Client / Brand** | *Café Delight* | — |
| **Project Description** | *6 Promotional Instagram Posts* | — |
| **Total Project Amount** | **₹2,000** | Total agreed quote |
| **Advance Amount (50%)** | **₹1,000** | 50% of total |
| **Advance Status** | `Received` | **Whether work can begin:** **YES** *(If Pending $\rightarrow$ DO NOT START)* |
| **Remaining Amount (50%)** | **₹1,000** | 50% balance |

#### Status Decision Summary:
* **Advance = Received?** $\rightarrow$ **Begin Design Work.**
* **Advance = Pending?** $\rightarrow$ **Wait. Do not start.**
* **Remaining = Received?** $\rightarrow$ **Deliver Final High-Res Files.**
* **Remaining = Pending?** $\rightarrow$ **Hold Final Files until payment is cleared.**
