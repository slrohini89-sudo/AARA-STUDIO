## 1. Objective of the Recurring Client System

The objective of Stage 10 is to transition satisfied one-time clients into steady, recurring monthly design clients. Instead of continuously hunting for new one-off projects, Riva Visuals provides dependable, ongoing visual design support to a small roster of regular businesses.

*Strict Boundary:*  
* This is purely a **design retainer**.
* Riva Visuals creates visual creatives; you do **not** manage accounts, post on their behalf, write full marketing strategies, or manage advertising campaigns.

---

## 2. When & How to Approach Satisfied Clients

* **Timing:** Shortly after delivering a successful one-time project when the client has expressed satisfaction and the final files are in hand.
* **Tone:** Natural, relaxed, friendly, and non-intimidating (never pushy).

### Client Conversion Message (WhatsApp Ready)
> *"Hey [Client Name], really enjoyed working on these designs with you! If you regularly need Instagram creatives, I can also handle your designs on a monthly basis so you have fresh, ready-to-post graphics whenever you need them. Let me know if that’s something that would be helpful for your brand!"*

## 6. Monthly Scope Management Rules for Solo Designers

1. **Retainer Payment in Advance:** Monthly retainer fees are paid at the start of each monthly billing cycle before design work for that month begins.
2. **Fixed Creative Quota:** Deliverables are capped strictly at the agreed package quantity (e.g., 8 posts, 4 stories, 1 carousel). Unused designs do not roll over indefinitely unless agreed.
3. **Dedicated Solo Capacity:** Working with 3–4 regular clients allows you to maintain top-tier visual quality without burning out or needing to hire employees.
