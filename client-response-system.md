---

## 1. Core Principles of Client Communication

* **Short & Clear:** Avoid long walls of text. Be direct, friendly, and professional.
* **Confidence without Desperation:** Maintain a calm, professional tone; do not sound needy.
* **No Premature Quoting:** Never quote a random price until you know the design format and quantity.
* **Design Focus Only:** Never promise follower growth, engagement boosts, or sales metrics. Riva Visuals provides visual design only.

---

## 2. The 4-Step Client Enquiry Flow

```
Step 1: Prospect Replies to Outreach
       ↓
Step 2: Qualify the Need (Ask 2 Core Questions)
       ↓
Step 3: Collect Basic Requirements
       ↓
Step 4: Present a Clear, Scope-Bound Quote + Next Step
```

---

## 3. Essential Questions to Ask Before Quoting

When a potential client replies showing interest or asking *"What are your charges?"*, use this exact question before throwing out a number:

> **"What kind of designs are you looking for, and approximately how many do you need?"**

This immediately clarifies:
1. **Design Type:** (e.g., Simple Post, Promotional Post, Carousel, Story, Poster, Reel Cover, etc.)
2. **Quantity:** (e.g., 1 single post, 5 posts, a set of stories, etc.)
3. **Scope & Turnaround:** What the project actually entails.

---

## 4. Collecting Key Requirements

Once they mention the general type and quantity, confirm these basic details:
* **The Topic/Subject:** What are the designs promoting? (e.g., a specific menu item, a seasonal discount, a service feature).
* **Content/Text Needed:** Do they have the exact text or offer details ready?
* **Assets:** Do they have high-res logos or product photos to provide?

---

## 5. Pricing Response & Quote Framework

When responding with a quote, keep it transparent and bounded:

### Standard Quote Formula
1. State the exact deliverable and quantity.
2. State the clear total price.
3. State what is included (initial designs + 1 revision per design).
4. State the delivery timeframe.
5. Offer a clear, low-friction next step.

### Example Script (Promotional Posts)
> *"For 6 promotional Instagram posts, the total would be ₹2,000.*  
> *This includes the initial custom designs, one revision per design, and delivery within 4–5 days.*  
> *If that works for you, let me know and we can get started on the details."*
