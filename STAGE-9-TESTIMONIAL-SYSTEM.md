## 1. Objective of the Testimonial System

The testimonial system provides a structured, ethical way to collect authentic feedback after completing client work. Real testimonials build credibility and trust with prospective clients.

*Strict Ethical Guardrail:*
* **Zero Fake Reviews:** Never invent quotes, names, ratings, or business results.
* If no testimonials exist yet, the reviews section remains empty or hidden until genuine client feedback is received.

---

## 2. When to Ask for a Testimonial

Timing is critical:
* **The Right Moment:** Only *after* the project is fully delivered, the balance is cleared, and the client confirms they are pleased with the final designs.
* **No Pressure:** The request must be low-pressure, polite, and completely optional.

---

## 3. The 6-Step Testimonial Workflow

```
1. Complete design deliverables and delivery
                   ↓
2. Confirm client satisfaction ("Glad you liked them!")
                   ↓
3. Send natural testimonial request message
                   ↓
4. Receive client's authentic feedback
                   ↓
5. Archive in genuine testimonial vault
                   ↓
6. Publish to Portfolio & Instagram "Reviews" Highlight
```

---

## 4. Client-Facing Request Message (WhatsApp Ready)

Send this concise, friendly message shortly after project handover:

> *"Hey [Client Name]! It was great working with you on this project. If you're happy with the designs, would you mind sharing a short testimonial about the work? It would mean a lot!"*
